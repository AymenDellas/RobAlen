Divorce does not pause your job

Deadlines still land. Staff still need answers. Clients still expect you to be sharp. The paperwork keeps arriving whether or not you have the headspace for it.

The hard part is rarely one big decision. It is the run of smaller ones, made while you are distracted, tired, and pulled in three directions at once.

That shows up as calls you put off, plans you leave half finished, and choices that normally take you ten minutes and now take a week.

Months can pass that way. The divorce moves forward regardless, and the work side of your life drifts further than it needed to.

A clear head, from someone focused on the practical side

Most divorce support is built around emotional processing. That has real value. It does not keep your business stable, your position coherent, or your judgement steady over the next stretch of months.

Strategic coaching picks up where that leaves off. It treats the months ahead as a set of decisions to be made well: what needs your attention now, what can wait, what to stop doing, and how to keep work moving while your personal life is in flux.

You bring the situation as it is. The work is turning it into something you can act on. It is the practical counterpart to emotional support, not a replacement for it.

The call is the starting point. You do not need to have anything organised before you book. Bring it as it stands, including the parts that are messy.

Two reasons men hesitate

If asking for help feels like an admission, reframe it. Getting structured support through a divorce is a business decision as much as a personal one. You would do the same if a work project was about to consume six months of your attention. The offer here is narrow: better judgement, a structure, and a plan for the months ahead.

The second hesitation is simpler. It is not clear what an initial conversation would actually be. So here is the honest version. It is a conversation about your situation and what you need from the next few months. You ask what you want to ask. You decide afterwards what happens next.
